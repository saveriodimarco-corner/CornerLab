# Provider cost ROI

## Estimated annual cost ranges

| Provider | Estimated annual cost | ROI view |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Sportradar | $75k-$200k | Attractive only if historical corner samples are strong and licence terms are favourable |
| Stats Perform | $60k-$180k | Attractive only if sample quality is proven |
| LSports | $40k-$150k | Moderate; depends on archive depth and fee structure |
| FeedConstruct | $35k-$120k | Moderate; depends on corner-specific archive availability |
| Betfair Historical Data | $25k-$100k | Potentially attractive if exchange-based closing prices are acceptable |

## Recommendation

Do not spend budget until a signed sample export demonstrates opening and closing Total Corners prices, bookmaker identity, timestamps and research rights. Without that evidence, ROI cannot be credibly estimated.
