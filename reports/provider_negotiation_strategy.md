# Provider negotiation strategy

1. Ask for a signed sample export covering at least three completed seasons of Serie A Total Corners odds.
2. Require opening and closing prices, bookmaker identity, fixture mapping keys, timestamps and settlement fields.
3. Make research rights explicit: internal research, model validation, backtesting, caching and derivative reporting.
4. Ask for a pilot pricing structure and a short-term trial before full annual commitment.
5. Keep a fallback plan: use public result data and manual odds archives only for initial validation, not production-grade model training.
